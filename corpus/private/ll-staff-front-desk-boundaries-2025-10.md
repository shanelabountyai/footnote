# What the front desk may and may not answer

The front desk is often the first voice a client hears. This memo draws the line so no one has to guess in the moment.

## Mechanics: yes
The front desk may restate how something works when the answer is written in a public IRS publication or a firm policy. Examples: what a form is called, which publication covers a topic, when the firm's office is open, how long the firm keeps records, what a mileage log must contain.

## Advice: no
The front desk may not say whether a client should or should not take a position, whether something is deductible for a particular client, or what a client's own file shows. Those answers need the engagement lead and, for money terms, the partner.

## How to decline
Say: "I can tell you what the publication says. For your situation I will ask the engagement lead to call you." Then log the call.

## When unsure
Treat the question as advice. A slow, correct hand-off costs a day; a wrong answer costs the client.
