# Record retention policy

## What the IRS says
Publication 15 says employers must keep employment tax records for at least 4 years. That floor covers payroll registers, deposit confirmations, and the filed quarterly returns. It is a minimum, and the firm treats it as the starting point rather than the finish line.

## What the firm does
Ledger & Lane keeps client records for 7 years from the close of the engagement year. The firm's own policy is longer than the IRS minimum on purpose: it covers the period in which a client may ask for an old workpaper, and it keeps one rule for every client instead of a different clock per document type.

## Handling
- Paper is scanned, then shredded after the scan is checked against the original.
- Electronic folders are archived at year end, read-only.
- Nothing is destroyed early without the partner signing the destruction log.
- When a client leaves, the 7 years still run from the last engagement year.

Questions about a specific client's folder go to the engagement lead, not the front desk.
