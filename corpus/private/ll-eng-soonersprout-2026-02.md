# Engagement memo — Sooner Sprout Nursery

## Profile
Sooner Sprout Nursery is a retail plant nursery with a seasonal greenhouse operation. The client's owner runs the business as a sole proprietorship. The firm provides monthly bookkeeping and a year-end package.

## Accounting method
Sooner Sprout Nursery uses the cash method of accounting.

## Fiscal year and publication
The fiscal year is the calendar year. For questions about what counts as a business expense, the firm points the client to Publication 535 and records which section the client was shown.

## Seasonal pattern
Sales peak from April through June and again in the autumn. Inventory is counted twice a year, once after the spring rush and once in late autumn. The count sheets are kept in the client folder.

## Open items
- Greenhouse supplier statements from last autumn are incomplete.
- The client's owner has not confirmed which card is used for personal purchases, so the bookkeeper is holding two transactions as unclassified.

## Reminder
This memo records facts. It does not tell the client how to treat any item.
