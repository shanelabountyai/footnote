# Client risk note — Prairie Wind Pet Grooming

## Why this note exists
The partner keeps a short risk note on any client where the firm's work could be affected by something outside the firm's control.

## Concerns
1. The shop depends on the client's owner personally. If the owner is out for a stretch, bookings and records both lapse.
2. The second member of the limited liability company is not active, and a recent request from that member came to the front desk rather than the named contact.
3. Records for the mobile appointments are thin before October.

## Assessment
The risk is moderate: low dollar value, but records are weak. The firm's scope is bookkeeping only, and the partner wants the engagement letter to say so plainly.

## Actions
- The engagement lead reminds the client's owner each quarter about the mileage log.
- Requests from the second member are held until the named contact approves.
- The partner reviews this note again at renewal.

This note records risk to the firm, not advice to the client.
