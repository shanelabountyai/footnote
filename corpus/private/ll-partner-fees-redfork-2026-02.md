# Fee note — Red Fork Welding LLC

## Decision
After the renewal call with the client's owner, the partner recorded the agreed terms below.

We agreed to a 15% discount on Red Fork Welding's monthly fee for the first six months.

## Why
The client's owner pointed to the added payroll work and the longer reconciliation cycle and asked for relief while the new process settles. The partner accepted for a fixed period rather than an open-ended change.

## Handling
- The discount applies to the monthly bookkeeping fee only, not to any one-off project.
- The engagement lead is told the client's rate is changing but does not need the percentage.
- The invoice template is updated by the partner, not the front desk.

## Review
At month six the partner revisits the fee. Until then the staff and engagement memos should not mention the arrangement. If a client asks the front desk about fees, the front desk refers them to the partner.
