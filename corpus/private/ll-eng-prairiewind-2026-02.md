# Engagement memo — Prairie Wind Pet Grooming

## Profile
Prairie Wind Pet Grooming is a two-chair grooming shop. The client's owner works in the shop and has one part-time groomer and a front-desk assistant. The client is a limited liability company taxed as a partnership with a second member who is not active in the shop.

## Method and year
The shop uses the cash method of accounting and a calendar fiscal year.

## Publications the firm uses
Publication 583 for record keeping, and Publication 463 when the client asks about vehicle logs, since the owner drives to mobile appointments two days a week.

## Open items
- The mobile appointment mileage log is incomplete before October.
- Booking software fees are charged to two different cards.

## Status
The bookkeeper reconciles monthly. The engagement lead will meet the client's owner at the end of the quarter to review the open items. This memo makes no statement about how any item should be reported.
