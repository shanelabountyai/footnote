# Note on late payroll deposits — Tallgrass HVAC

## What happened
Two payroll deposits for Tallgrass HVAC were made late during the fourth quarter. The confirmations show the dates, and the bookkeeper caught the pattern during the monthly reconciliation.

## Cause as the firm understands it
The client's office lead was approving payroll runs on the day of the deposit rather than the day before, and a bank holiday pushed one deposit past its due date. Publication 15 sets the deposit schedules; the firm's job is to record what happened, not to argue with the schedule.

## Firm action
- The engagement lead told the client's owner what the confirmations show.
- The firm now sends a reminder message two business days before each deposit date.
- The partner decided that the firm will not make deposits for the client; that remains the client's responsibility.

## Exposure to the firm
The firm's exposure is limited so long as the engagement letter keeps deposits with the client. The partner wants that wording confirmed at renewal.

## Follow-up
The partner reviews the next two quarters of confirmations before closing the note.
