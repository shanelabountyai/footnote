# Fee negotiation note — Blue Dome Design Studio

## Context
Blue Dome's office lead asked for a lower monthly fee after the studio lost a large contract. The partner met the client's owner and the office lead together.

## What was discussed
The studio proposed a discount of ten percent for the winter months. The partner declined a percentage and offered instead to hold the current fee flat through the next fiscal year, with no increase.

## Outcome
The client's owner accepted the flat fee. No discount was given. The partner wrote the agreement on a single page and filed it with the engagement letter.

## Reasoning for the file
The partner prefers a flat commitment to a discount because a discount tends to become the new price. The note is a record of the conversation, not a rule for other clients.

## Follow-up
Revisit at the start of the next fiscal year. The engagement lead was told only that the fee stays the same.
