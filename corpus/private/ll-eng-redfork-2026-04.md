# Records request note — Red Fork Welding LLC

## What was asked
The client's owner asked the firm for copies of the past two years of payroll registers and deposit confirmations, to hand to a lender.

## What the firm did
The bookkeeper pulled the registers from the archived payroll folder and checked each page against the file index. The deposit confirmations were exported from the payroll portal and saved as PDF files under the usual naming convention.

## Handling rules applied
- Only the client's owner may receive the files, and only through the portal.
- Account numbers on the confirmations were left as stored; the portal link expires after seven days.
- The request and the date of delivery were logged in the folder.

## Still open
The lender also wanted a letter describing the firm's role. The engagement lead has drafted a short factual description of the services in the engagement letter. The partner has to approve any wording that goes outside the firm, and that approval is not yet recorded.
