# Records request note — Tallgrass HVAC

## The request
The client's owner asked for copies of last year's payroll deposit confirmations, to answer a question from the client's insurance carrier about its employee count.

## Steps taken
The bookkeeper retrieved the confirmations for all four quarters from the archive and checked each against the payroll register. One confirmation was missing from the archive and was re-exported from the payroll portal. The re-export was noted in the folder log so the gap is explained.

## Release
Files were sent only to the client's owner through the portal. The firm did not send the employee list because the carrier did not ask for it.

## Retention reminder
The firm's own record-retention policy governs how long these confirmations stay in the archive; this note does not change it.

## Open
The carrier may ask for a letter. If so, the partner approves the wording before it leaves the firm.
