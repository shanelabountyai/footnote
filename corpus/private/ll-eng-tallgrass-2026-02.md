# Engagement memo — Tallgrass HVAC

## Profile
Tallgrass HVAC installs and services heating and cooling systems for homes and small commercial buildings. It is a corporation with eleven employees, including four field technicians. The firm provides monthly bookkeeping and payroll support.

## Method and year
Tallgrass HVAC uses the accrual method of accounting and a calendar fiscal year. Work in progress on larger installs is tracked job by job in the ledger.

## Publications
Publication 15 for employer deposits and filings, Publication 463 for the technicians' vehicle logs, and Publication 946 when the client asks about equipment such as service vans.

## Open items
- Fuel card statements for two months are missing.
- Several technicians' vehicle logs are incomplete.
- Warranty parts credits from a supplier have not been matched.

## Status
The bookkeeper reconciles monthly and flags exceptions to the engagement lead. This memo does not take any position for the client.
