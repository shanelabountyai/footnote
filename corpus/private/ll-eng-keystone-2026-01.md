# Engagement memo — Keystone Kolache Co.

## Profile
Keystone Kolache Co. is a small bakery with a retail counter and a wholesale route to local cafes. It is organized as a corporation and has six employees. The firm provides monthly bookkeeping, payroll support and a quarterly review.

## Method and year
The bakery uses the accrual method of accounting with a fiscal year ending on the last day of June. The firm's reference for fiscal years and accounting methods is Publication 538.

## Employment taxes
Payroll is the most sensitive area. Publication 15 is the firm's reference for employer deposit schedules, and the bookkeeper confirms each deposit against the portal receipt.

## Open items
- Wholesale invoices for December were issued late and are being matched to deposits.
- Flour and packaging vendors changed names, so the vendor list needs cleanup.

## Note
The client has asked for the quarterly review earlier in the month. The engagement lead will confirm a date.
