# Pricing for next year

## Starting point
The firm's monthly bookkeeping fees have not changed in two years. Costs for software, the portal and the shared storage have risen, and busy-season overtime is up.

## Proposed schedule
- Monthly bookkeeping: increase by a modest flat amount per client, not a percentage.
- Payroll support: priced per pay run, with a minimum.
- Quarterly review: unchanged.
- Year-end package: a small increase tied to the number of accounts.

## Client groups
Smaller clients with simple books get the lower end of the range. Clients with job costing or multiple entities get the upper end. The partner will explain the schedule to each client by phone before any letter goes out.

## Exceptions
Any exception or discount is the partner's decision, written down, and kept in this tier. Staff are not told individual rates.

## Timing
Letters go out in May for fees that begin with the new fiscal year. The engagement leads get a one-page summary the week before.
