# Records request note — Sooner Sprout Nursery

## The request
An outside insurer asked the client to show last year's sales and inventory count sheets. The client's owner forwarded the request to the firm and asked what could be shared.

## What the firm provided
The bookkeeper assembled three items from the client folder: the monthly sales summaries, the two inventory count sheets, and the year-end package cover page. Each was reviewed against the file index before release.

## Not provided
Bank statements, payroll reports and the engagement letter were left out because the insurer did not ask for them. The firm shares only what was requested and only with the client's owner, who decides whether to pass it on.

## Logging
The request, the items released, and the delivery date are logged in the folder. The delivery went through the portal with a seven-day expiry.

## Open
The insurer may ask a follow-up. If it does, the engagement lead handles it.
