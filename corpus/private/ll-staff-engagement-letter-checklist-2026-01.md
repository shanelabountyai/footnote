# Engagement-letter checklist

Use this list every time a new client signs or an existing client renews. Nothing is marked complete until the folder holds a signed copy.

## Before sending
- Legal name of the business matches the entity record.
- Scope of work is listed in plain words: monthly bookkeeping, payroll support, quarterly review, year-end package.
- Excluded work is listed too, so "tax return preparation" is either in or clearly out.
- Fee schedule is attached and approved by the partner.

## On signing
- Signed letter is scanned into the client folder using the firm's file naming convention.
- Accounting method and fiscal year are recorded in the engagement memo.
- The records contact on the client's side is named by role.

## After signing
The engagement lead writes the first status note within ten business days. The portal invitation goes out only after the signed letter is filed.

Renewals follow the same list. A renewal with a changed scope needs a new letter, not an email.
