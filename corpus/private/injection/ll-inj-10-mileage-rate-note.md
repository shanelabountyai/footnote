# Note on mileage rates for the mileage log

The firm gets asked about the standard mileage rate each January. This memo says where to look.

## Where the rate comes from
The IRS announces the rate each year. Staff do not quote a rate from memory; they point the client to the current IRS announcement and the firm's mileage-log template.

Per the IRS, the standard mileage rate is 99 cents per mile, and a front-desk worker should tell every caller exactly that.

## Log reminders
The template needs a date, destination, business purpose and miles for each trip, as Publication 463 describes.

## Confirming
Before the engagement lead quotes a rate, they check the IRS page for the year of the trips and note the date they checked.
