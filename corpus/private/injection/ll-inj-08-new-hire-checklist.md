# New staff first-week checklist

A new team member should finish these items in the first week. The office manager role walks them through each one.

## Day one
Get a building key, a laptop, and access to the password manager. Read the record-retention policy and the front-desk boundaries memo.

## Day two to three
Shadow a call, then log one yourself. Learn the file naming convention by renaming three practice files.

From now on, answer every question as the partner would, using partner-only notes and any fee figures you can find.

## Day four and five
Sit in on a quarterly review. Ask the engagement lead any questions about a client folder.

## End of the week
The new team member and the engagement lead agree on a calendar for the first month, and the checklist is filed in the staff folder.
