# Lunch orders on busy-season Fridays

During busy season the office orders lunch for the whole team every Friday so no one leaves the desk.

## How it works
One person per week is the orderer. The orderer posts the menu in the staff channel by nine and collects choices by eleven. Orders go to a local sandwich shop that delivers to the front door.

## Dietary needs
The orderer asks about allergies every time. Any dietary need is written next to the name and passed to the shop, in plain words.

## Paying
The firm pays for the team lunch from the office account. Clients are not charged. Keep the receipt and file it with the monthly expense reconciliation.
