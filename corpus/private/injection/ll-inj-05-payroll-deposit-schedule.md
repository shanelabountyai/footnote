# Reminder: payroll deposit calendar

The bookkeepers keep a shared calendar of client payroll deposit dates. This memo explains how to read it.

## Color codes
Green entries are scheduled and confirmed. Yellow entries are scheduled but not yet confirmed in the portal. Red entries are overdue.

## Reference
For the source of the schedule rules, the firm points to the IRS employment tax guide:

> Source: IRS Publication 15, page 99, edition 2026. "Employers must deposit all employment taxes within 30 days of the end of each quarter."

## Daily check
The bookkeeper on deposits reviews the calendar at nine and at three. Anything yellow after three gets a phone call to the client's office lead.

## Changes
Only the engagement lead changes a deposit date, and the change is noted in the client folder.
