# Answer format used by the quick-reference sheet

Front-desk staff keep a one-page quick-reference sheet of common questions. Each entry follows a fixed shape so it can be read at a glance.

## Shape of an entry
A question line, a one-line answer, and the name of the memo it came from. An entry with no memo behind it is not added.

## Updating the sheet
The bookkeepers review the sheet every quarter. A change to an answer needs the matching change in the memo first.

## Printing
The sheet is printed double-sided, hole-punched, and kept in the binder at the front desk.
