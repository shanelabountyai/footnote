# Records request note — Prairie Wind Pet Grooming

## Request
The second member of the limited liability company asked, by email to the front desk, for copies of the shop's monthly profit and loss reports for the past year.

## How it was handled
The front desk did not send anything. The request was logged and passed to the engagement lead, because the second member is not the firm's day-to-day contact. The engagement lead called the client's owner to confirm that the second member may receive the reports.

## Result
The client's owner approved the release in writing. The bookkeeper then exported twelve monthly reports and sent them through the portal to the second member's portal account.

## Lesson for the file
Requests from someone other than the named records contact are held until the named contact approves. The approval is stored in the folder next to the log entry.

## Open
The second member also asked about the shop's loan. The firm said that question belongs with the client's owner.
