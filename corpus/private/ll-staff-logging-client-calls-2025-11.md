# How to log a client call

Every client call gets a log entry before the next call is picked up. The log is a short form in the shared tracker, and it exists so that anyone covering the phones can see what was said last.

## What goes in the entry
1. Client name, as it appears in the file naming convention.
2. Date and time the call began.
3. Who called, by role only: the client's owner, the client's office lead, a vendor.
4. One sentence on the reason.
5. What the firm said, in plain words. If the answer was "I will pass this to the engagement lead", write exactly that.
6. Follow-up owner and due date.

## What stays out
No account numbers, no tax identification numbers, no copies of what the client read aloud. If the caller offers a number, ask them to send it through the portal instead.

## Closing the loop
A follow-up that is not closed within two business days gets flagged at the Monday check-in. Closed means the client heard back, not that someone meant to reply.
