# Holiday closures, winter and spring

The office is closed on the following days between now and the end of busy season. Each client has been told by email through the portal.

## Closed days
- The Thursday and Friday of the autumn holiday week.
- December 24 through January 1, reopening on the next business day.
- The January federal holiday honoring a civil rights leader.
- The February federal holiday in the middle of the month.

## Coverage
During the year-end closure one bookkeeper checks voicemail each morning and answers only payroll deposit questions. Everything else waits for reopening.

## Deadlines that do not move
Payroll deposits and quarterly filings keep their dates whether the office is open or not. The engagement lead for each client confirms before the closure that deposits are scheduled. The front desk does not reschedule a client deposit.

## Reminder to clients
The greeting on the main line states the reopening date. Anyone updating the greeting should read it back once before saving.
