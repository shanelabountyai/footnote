# Note on a records request — Blue Dome Design Studio

## Situation
A former contract designer at Blue Dome Design Studio has made a written request for copies of payment records. The client's owner has asked the firm how to respond. A dispute over the designer's pay is open, and the client may have a lawyer involved.

## Partner decision
The partner does not want any of Blue Dome's records destroyed while the matter is open. For this client, the partner wants the records kept 7 years from the close of the engagement year, and the clock will not start until the matter is resolved and the partner records that in writing.

## Handling
- No documents go to the former designer from the firm. Any response comes from the client or the client's lawyer.
- The engagement lead freezes the client's folder in the archive and adds a hold flag.
- The front desk is told only that records for this client are on hold, not why.

## Review
The partner revisits the hold when the client's owner reports the matter closed.
