# Office hours and phone coverage

The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:30 in the morning to 5:00 in the afternoon, Central time. The front desk is covered for the full span, with a one-hour midday gap when a bookkeeper takes the phone.

## Phones
Calls ring first at the front desk. After four rings they roll to whichever bookkeeper is marked "on phones" on the shared board. If no one is marked, the call goes to voicemail, and the voicemail is checked at ten, noon and four.

## Busy season
From the first of March through the middle of April, the firm adds Saturday morning hours, nine to noon. Only the on-call bookkeeper is in on those mornings, and the front desk is not staffed. Clients are told to leave a message.

## After hours
There is no after-hours line. The voicemail greeting states the next business day and says the firm does not take urgent requests by text. Anyone who finds a stale greeting should tell the office manager role the same day.
