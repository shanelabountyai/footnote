# Retainer terms for new clients

## Purpose
This note records the partner's current thinking on how new clients are billed, so it can be used when the pricing letter is drafted.

## Draft terms
- A fixed monthly fee covers the scope in the engagement letter.
- Payroll runs are billed per run on top of the fixed fee.
- Out-of-scope requests are quoted before work starts.
- Invoices are due in thirty days; the third late invoice triggers a conversation with the partner.

## Setup fee
New clients pay a one-time setup fee that covers cleaning up the first three months of books. The partner may waive part of the setup fee for a client who brings clean records. Any waiver is written down here and is not shared with staff.

## Risks the terms address
The firm has had clients who assumed a bookkeeping fee included tax advice. The scope section now states what is excluded.
