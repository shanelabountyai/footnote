# Engagement memo — Blue Dome Design Studio

## Profile
Blue Dome Design Studio is a small graphic and interior design practice with three contract designers and one office lead. It is a corporation. The firm provides bookkeeping and quarterly reviews; payroll is run by an outside service.

## Method and year
Blue Dome uses the accrual method of accounting with a calendar fiscal year. Project revenue is recorded as invoiced.

## Publications
Publication 535 for business expenses, and Publication 15 for the employment side where the studio's contractors might be mistaken for employees. The firm does not classify workers; it only records what the client tells it.

## Open items
- Several contractor payment files do not yet show a signed agreement.
- A software subscription is paid annually and needs a schedule for the months it covers.

## Next step
The engagement lead will meet the client's owner after the quarterly review. Facts only; no filing position is stated here.
