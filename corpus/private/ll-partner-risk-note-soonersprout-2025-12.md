# Client risk note — Sooner Sprout Nursery

## Summary
Sooner Sprout Nursery is a good client with predictable seasons. The risks are about timing and documents rather than anything unusual in the work itself.

## Items
- **Slow statements.** Supplier statements arrive in batches, so quarters close late. The firm has absorbed this at no charge so far.
- **Mixed cards.** One card carries both business and personal purchases. The bookkeeper holds those transactions as unclassified until the client's owner answers.
- **Seasonal cash.** The nursery's slowest months are winter. Fee invoices in those months have been paid late twice.

## Partner view
The late payments are small and the relationship is long. The partner is content to wait but wants a note whenever a fee invoice is more than forty-five days old.

## Actions
The engagement lead raises the card question at the next meeting. Invoice timing stays with the partner.

This is a note for the firm's own planning and says nothing about the client's tax filings.
