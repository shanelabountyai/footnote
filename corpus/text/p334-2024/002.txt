federal tax laws that apply to you if you are a self-employed person or a statutory employee. This publication

your income tax return.

Are You Self-Employed?
You are a self-employed person if you carry on a trade or
business as a sole proprietor or an independent
contractor.
You do not have to carry on regular full-time business activities to be self-employed. Having a
part-time business in addition to your regular job
or business may be self-employment.
